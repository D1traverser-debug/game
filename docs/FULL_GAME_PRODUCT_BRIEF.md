# Full-Game Product Brief

## 1. Product intent

Build a complete independent game that can replace The Spell Brigade for players who want its core survivors-like spell-combat loop, build crafting, elemental interactions, co-op chaos, objective pressure and boss progression. The reference game is used for observable gameplay research only. The shipped product must have its own title, world, characters, art, UI, music, sound and writing.

## 2. Product promise

"Four spellcasters enter a living magical frontier. Every spell can reshape the battlefield, every element changes the build, and every ally can become an accidental hazard. Survive the mission, complete team objectives, break the boss, and bring discoveries back to camp."

This is a draft positioning line, not final marketing copy.

## 3. Modes and platform

- Solo mode and online co-op for 2–4 players.
- Windows PC first, keyboard/mouse and controller.
- Steam distribution target; no claim that Steamworks, servers or publishing are configured.
- Console, cross-platform play and handheld certification are later gates.
- Native Unity build. The old browser HTML is a mechanics prototype, not the final product.

## 4. Gameplay pillars

1. **Readable chaos:** high enemy counts and spectacular spell interactions, but players can still read danger and react.
2. **Build expression:** spell identities, levels, augmentations, seven elemental identities and pair interactions create meaningful builds.
3. **Co-op tension:** friendly fire, revives, shared objectives and player-specific strengths make teamwork powerful but risky.
4. **Run pressure:** objectives, waves, map hazards, elites and bosses create a changing mission rather than a static survival timer.
5. **Persistent discovery:** quests, unlocks, camp upgrades and meta progression give runs long-term purpose.

## 5. Reference scope inventory

The working research target is:
- 15 wizards (community inventory; verify current version).
- Four realms: Verdant Meadows, Frozen Ancients, Pyrestorm Pit and Astral Riftlands.
- An evidence-reconciled primary-element and advanced-infusion catalogue. Current sources conflict: the detailed matrix lists five primary elements plus fifteen advanced infusion candidates, while another community guide summarizes seven identities and ten combinations. Resolve against the pinned current in-game version before locking counts.
- Full spell catalogue and augmentations, including official 1.2 additions.
- Enemy/boss rosters, objective catalogue and variants, scrolls, chests, quests, trials/covenants, enchantments/masteries, wizard rank, cosmetics and achievements.
- Solo and 1–4 player online co-op, including friendly fire and revive rules.

The official update snapshot currently located is 1.1 Realm Sweep (2026-09-02), 1.2.0.20127 (2026-10-06), and the performance patch (2026-10-22). Recheck for a later major update before locking the content manifest.

## 6. Original IP and art direction

The product will not use The Spell Brigade's name, logo, wizard identities, character/creature designs, exact UI expression, music, audio recordings, text, animation or extracted assets. Create an original magical world and coherent visual language. Functional gameplay behavior may be independently implemented from observed mechanics.

## 7. Quality bar

A polished vertical slice must demonstrate:
- A memorable original hero with a production-quality model, rig, animation set and signature spell.
- One fully dressed realm with a modular environment kit, lighting, terrain materials, props, ambience and navigation.
- Three distinct spells with visible differences in launch, travel, impact, damage feedback and audio.
- One elemental interaction with clear mechanical and audiovisual feedback.
- Three enemy archetypes with locomotion, attacks, hit reactions, death and drops.
- One objective with clear state feedback.
- One boss with authored telegraphs, active hitboxes, stagger/recovery and a natural victory path.
- High-quality HUD, level-up/upgrade UI, pause, settings and results.
- Original adaptive music and complete core SFX.
- Solo and 1–4 player networked sessions with stable state and readable combat.
- Target performance on frozen reference hardware.

## 8. Non-goals for first production slice

- No mass production of all 15 heroes before the first slice passes.
- No console/cross-play commitment before PC networking is proven.
- No placeholder-only full roster presented as finished content.
- No copied proprietary assets or unlicensed third-party packs.
- No claim of source-perfect numeric fidelity where source evidence is missing.
